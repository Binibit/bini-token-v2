# Known Limitations

1. Unknown, future and custom AMMs are not universally detected.
2. A CREATE2 address may be funded before pool code exists.
3. Registry completeness and factory authenticity are governance duties.
4. Timelock governance can upgrade the token and modify policy after delay.
5. Emergency pause is a trusted immediate capability.
6. Phase 1 Sepolia and independent external audit are not complete.
7. Branch protection is unavailable on the current private-repository plan.
