# Product Canon

BINI is `Binibit`, symbol `BINI`, 18 decimals and a fixed one-time supply of
1,000,000,000 BINI minted to the Genesis Distribution Safe. It has no runtime
mint, burn, tax, blacklist, participant allowlist or forced transfer.

Ordinary transfers are free in `PRE_MARKET`. The token blocks supported V2/V3
pools discovered through approved factories and explicitly registered DEX
infrastructure. `openMarket()` is Timelock-only, one-way and independent from
emergency pause.
