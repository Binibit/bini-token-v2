# BINI V2 Phase 2 Migration Backlog

Phase 2 is independent from Phase 1 and requires separate owner authorization.

- Ratify canonical V1 contract and prove 12 decimals/code hash.
- Ratify deployed V2 proxy and immutable Migration Vault addresses.
- Obtain independent external audit of vault, holder authorization, and custody.
- Freeze approximately 50 known-holder records and SHA-256 manifest.
- Verify ownership evidence, replacement-recipient signatures, and Safe records.
- Ratify exact migration reserve amount and funding source from an approved Safe.
- Prove no Phase 1 allocation is silently reclassified as a tenth pool.
- Define start/end communications, support, incident, and accounting procedures.
- Rehearse entitlement setup, funding, sealing, migration, replay prevention,
  fee-on-transfer rejection, global pause interaction, and reconciliation on
  Sepolia.
- Archive immutable config, holder file, Timelock/Safe packages, receipts, and
  `verify-migration` evidence.
- Design individual Team/Founder vesting, Rewards budgets, Marketing milestones,
  Ecosystem grants, and their own audited contracts as separate detailed
  distribution work.

Do not modify the token supply or add minting to fund migration.
